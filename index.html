What is RootTap? I'm glad you asked!

RootTap, a fun and engaging clicker game where players tap to plant and grow trees, building their very own thriving forest. Starting with a single sapling, players unlock new species of trees, upgrade their resources, and grow a lush forest ecosystem. Here is what we seek to accomplish with the game:

  • Raise Environmental Awareness through Gaming
  • Promote Sustainable Behavior
  • Further Support Local Green Projects
  • Raise Awareness for Deforestation
  • Making a Real Change

Unlike most traditional clicker games, RootTap is more than just a virtual experience. It’s a game that fosters real-world environmental awareness and action. Players aren't just clicking for rewards; they’re building a thriving forest and learning about the importance of trees, forests, and sustainability. As players grow their virtual forest, they’re also encouraged to take part in real-world eco-actions.
